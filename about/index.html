Under one roof

Four workshops.
One building.

Most places that sell you a uniform, a sticker and a flyer are buying at least two of those from somebody else. We're not. That's why your branding actually matches across all three.

Made right here.
Design studio

In-house graphic designers who set up artwork, build brand identities and rebuild the old stuff that's gone fuzzy.

Commercial embroidery

Industrial Barudan machine, digitising done properly, thread matched to your Pantones or picked from our chart in store.

Garment printing

DTG and DTF in-house on certified water-based inks — short runs, unlimited colours, no minimum for transfers.

Wide format & vinyl

Printing to 1.3 metres wide, premium vinyls cut daily, plus fine art giclee on archival media.

We're a shop, not a portal

There's a real front door at 44 McBean Street with real people behind it. You can come in, hold the vinyl, feel the difference between a DTG print and an embroidered logo, flick through the fabric and mesh swatches for a custom cap, and decide with your hands instead of a thumbnail.

We're a busy walk-in showroom as well as a production workshop, which is why the fastest way to get something sorted is often to call in. And it's why our quotes come from someone who's actually seen the job — not a form that spits out a number.

We prefer the personal approach. No fancy online designer, no algorithm laying out your logo. A real person designs your gear, matches your colours and reviews it before it's produced.

Why in-house design matters

Flyers, stickers, custom apparel and merch all need to be designed with purpose. Colour, size, placement and the unglamorous rules of layout are what make a concept land — instead of looking like it was thrown together on the way out the door.

With in-house designers and an experienced production team, your job only goes to print once we're happy it meets our specs. That's the difference between us and the big online companies running a press-print service: they print what lands in the inbox, we fix it first.

We're a tight-knit team working across every department, so consistency is always achieved. Your colours stay your colours whether they end up on paper, on a sticker, on a stubby cooler or stitched into a cap.

  • Brand identity
  • Rebuilding old artwork
  • Pantone matching
  • Cross-product consistency

What we're like to deal with

The stuff we
won't budge on.

Not a poster in the lunchroom. This is genuinely how the shop runs, and it explains most of the answers you'll get from us.

We don't do ugly.

We push the boundaries

Anything but ordinary. If your idea is safe, we'll tell you — and then we'll show you the version that stops traffic. Boring is a choice, and it's rarely a good one.

More than just products

You're not buying a shirt, a cap or a sticker. You're buying visibility, consistency and a brand that looks like it belongs to a business that knows what it's doing.

We'll reject an ugly idea

Our design team has no hesitation in saying no to something that won't work. Politely. Usually with a better option attached. It's your name on it, and ours too.

Quality before price

We'd rather quote honestly on something that lasts than win the job on a number and hand you something that fades, peels or falls apart in a Capricorn Coast summer.

No shortcuts

Logos get digitised properly. Files get checked before they go to press. Placements get measured. It's slower on some jobs and it's the whole reason the results hold up.

Depth of experience

Print, stickers, embroidery, garment printing and promo, in one team. That range is what lets us tell you which method your job actually needs — not just the one we happen to sell.
