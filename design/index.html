Good to know

Common questions.

Do I have to use your design service?
Not at all. Supply print-ready artwork and we'll produce from it. But we don't do free artwork checks — if files need setup or fixing to work in production, that's design time and we'll quote it before we touch anything. Ask us for an artwork spec sheet before you send files and you'll usually avoid it entirely.
What files should I supply?
Adobe Illustrator vector artwork is our preference wherever you have it, with fonts supplied or converted to outlines. High-resolution images are acceptable. For print, a press-quality PDF with embedded fonts, 300dpi images and 3mm bleed is ideal. Web-sized JPEGs and screenshots usually need rebuilding.
Can you fix my old logo?
Yes. Rebuilding low-resolution or damaged artwork as clean vector files is one of the most common jobs we do. Send us whatever you've got — even if it's a photo of an old shirt — and we'll tell you what's involved.
Do you design logos from scratch?
Logo design is currently taken by application only. Get in touch with what you're after and we'll be straight with you about whether we can take it on. If we can't, we can still help with everything that goes around it.
When do I pay for design work?
Design is invoiced before our team starts. Once your project is on board, the design team will walk you through exactly how the process runs and what's included.
Do I get to see it before it's made?
Yes. Once you've placed your order we prepare an art proof and nothing is produced until you've approved it. Check the details, sizes, quantities and spelling carefully at that point — changes after approval push your job back into the design queue. See how it works for the full process.
Can you keep my branding consistent across everything?
That's exactly why we run design in-house. We hold your files and colour references, so your uniforms, stickers, merch and print all look like they belong to the same business — because the same team is making all of them.
What if my idea is a bit ordinary?
We'll tell you. Our team has no hesitation in rejecting ugly ideas, and we'll usually come back with an alternative rather than just a no. It's your name going on it — and ours going on the invoice.
