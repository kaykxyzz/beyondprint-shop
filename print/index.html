Good to know

Common questions.

Can you design it, or do I have to supply artwork?
Either. Supply a print-ready file and we'll check it before it goes anywhere near the press, or hand the whole thing to our in-house design team. Most of our print work is designed here. See our design and branding services for the full picture.
What file should I send?
A press-quality PDF with fonts embedded, images at 300dpi and 3mm bleed is ideal. If that sentence meant nothing to you, just send what you've got — we'll tell you what needs fixing and quote the file setup if it needs it.
Do I get to see a proof?
Yes. Once you've placed your order we prepare an art proof and nothing prints until you've signed it off. That's your chance to check spelling, phone numbers and colours — we check too, but you know your business better than we do.
How big can you actually print?
Wide format goes up to 1.3 metres wide and effectively any length, which covers banners and oversize posters. Anything wider is usually better handled as panels — we'll tell you which way to go when we quote.
Can you match colours to my existing branding?
Yes, and this is one of the real advantages of doing everything under one roof. We hold your brand colours and files so your flyers, stickers, uniforms and merch all look like they belong to the same business.
Do you print small runs, or is there a minimum?
Digital printing means short runs are genuinely viable — fifty flyers, a hundred cards, a single poster. Quantity affects unit cost rather than whether we'll do it. Tell us the number you actually need and we'll quote that.
How long will my print job take?
It depends on the job, the finishing and how quickly the proof is approved — a digital flyer run and a bound docket book order are very different animals. We'll give you a realistic timeframe with your quote instead of a number that sounds impressive and then slips.

Ready to put your brand on paper?

Tell us what you need, how many, and when it has to be in hand. We'll come back with options and honest pricing.
