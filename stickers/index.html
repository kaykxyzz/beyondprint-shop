Good to know

Common questions.

What's the difference between die-cut and kiss-cut?
Die-cut stickers are cut all the way through, so each one comes away as an individual shape. Kiss-cut stickers are cut through the vinyl but not the backing, so they sit on a sheet you can peel from — better for sets, packing into orders, and anything you want to hand over as a single item.
Can I get any shape I like?
Yes. Circles, squares, rounded rectangles, your logo silhouette, a fish, a dog, a spanner — the contour cutter follows whatever outline the artwork gives it. Very fine points and hairline detail are the only real limit, and we'll flag it if your shape is heading that way.
Will they survive outdoors?
The outdoor and vehicle stickers we print use UV-stable vinyl and are laminated for abrasion and weather resistance. Marine work uses premium marine-rated vinyl. Tell us where the sticker is going — dishwasher, ute tailgate, fuel tank, boat hull, freezer — and we'll spec the right stock rather than guessing.
Do you print small quantities?
Yes. Digital printing makes short runs genuinely worthwhile, so a handful of boat name decals or a single sheet is no problem. Quantity affects your unit cost, not whether we'll take the job.
Can you design the sticker for me?
That's what the in-house design team is for. Send a rough idea, a photo, or nothing at all — we'll build the artwork, set the cut line properly and keep it consistent with the rest of your branding. See our design and branding services.
How do I supply artwork?
Vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) are ideal because they scale cleanly and give us a clean cut path. High-resolution PNGs with a transparent background work for most jobs too. Send what you've got and we'll tell you honestly whether it'll hold up at the size you want.
Do I see a proof before you print?
Yes. Once your order is placed we prepare an art proof showing the artwork, the size and the cut line, and nothing prints until you approve it.
Can you supply on rolls for a labelling machine?
We can. Let us know the core size, roll direction and whether they're being hand-applied or machine-applied, and we'll produce them to suit. If you're not sure, bring the machine details in and we'll work it out with you.

Got something that needs sticking?

Tell us the shape, the size, the quantity and where it's going. We'll come back with options and honest pricing.
