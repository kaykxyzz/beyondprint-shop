Direct to Garment (DTG) Photo-accurate, unlimited-colour artwork on cotton tees. Best suited to short runs under 100. Small orders welcome Water-based Innobella inks, 1200×1200 dpi, 2-pass for colour load. Softer hand than screen print but a finer ink layer — expect around 30 washes with correct care, and 10–20% fade on white tees, 5–10% on coloured. We print DTG on our tested AS Colour range only, and we don't print white ink on white garments.
Direct to Film (DTF) Unlimited colours on almost anything — tees, polos, caps, bags, pants and stretchy fabrics. No minimum (multiples are better value) 1200 dpi ultra-fine detail, soft to touch, great stretchability, and ultra durable. Heat-applied, so skip the clothes dryer. Over roughly 150 units, talk to us about screen printing instead.
Embroidery Logos on caps, polos, workwear, hi-vis, bags and corporate uniforms. One-off digitising fee per logo Stitched in-house on our Barudan industrial machine. Nothing lasts longer — it will likely outlast the shirt. Lettering has a minimum height of 5–6 mm, gradients aren't possible, and very fine detail has limits. Pantone matching recommended.
Supaflex Detailed logos and full-colour designs that need to land with impact. Ask us — varies by garment Highly durable badge application. A good middle ground when embroidery can't hold the detail.
Supaetch Highly detailed logos with a natural, understated finish. Bags, caps, blankets, beanies and scarves. Ask us — varies by garment Eleven suede colours, with your choice of matching or contrast thread. Subtle branding with a high perceived value and a retail look.
Badges & patches Woven or leather patches on caps and a limited range of garments. Higher minimums apply A premium, tactile finish. Available on selected items only — check with us before you plan around it.
Sublimation Fully custom fishing shirts, polos and sportswear where the whole garment is the canvas. 10 shirts (short and long sleeve counted separately) No colour limits at all. Premium UV-rated fabric and quick-dry sportsmesh. Minimum four weeks from payment because the garments are manufactured to your design — this is not a fast option.

Good to know

Common questions.

What's the minimum order for custom sublimated shirts?
Ten shirts. Short sleeve and long sleeve designs count as separate orders, so the minimum of ten applies to each.
How long do custom sublimated shirts take?
Most orders take a minimum of four weeks once your payment has been processed. Complex designs with detailed artwork or a lot of colours take longer because of the print sampling process, and international holidays observed by our manufacturers can affect it further.
Can we get a mixed order?
You can have as many different sizes of the same shirt as you like in one order. Short sleeve and long sleeve are treated as separate orders, so each needs to hit the minimum of ten.
How do we design the shirt?
Our in-house design team handles it. You supply your logos and any images you want used, and we do the rest — then you approve the design before anything goes into production.
Why is there a charge to digitise my logo for embroidery?
Because we have to digitally map every individual stitch, then test-stitch and adjust it to compensate for the fabric type. It's a one-time fee — once your logo is digitised, it's ready for every future order.
Can I bring in my own garments to be decorated?
We don't typically accept customer-supplied items. If we do agree to it, they must be brand new and unworn, and a customer-supplied goods fee applies per garment. We supply a wide range of headwear and garments suitable for both printing and embroidery.
How should I wash printed garments?
Cold gentle wash inside out, no tumble drying, no direct sunlight, and absolutely no fabric softener or bleach — that strips the print. Iron inside out if you must. Follow that and your print should last around 30 washes.
Can you match my exact brand colours?
For embroidery, supply your Pantone colours and we'll match to them, or choose in store from our thread chart — bear in mind thread has a high sheen so colours can read more intense than expected. For garment printing, a colour match is a service we add to your job if accuracy really matters.

Ready to get your team looking sharp?

Tell us the garment, the quantity and roughly what you want on it. We'll come back with options and honest pricing.
