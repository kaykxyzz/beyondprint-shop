Step by step

The long version.

Read this once and your job will run smoother than 90% of the ones that come through the door.

Step 1

Submit an enquiry

Every service page has an enquiry form on it, and each one lands with the department that actually makes the thing. If you're after several different products, send separate enquiries — apparel, print and stickers are made by different people at different benches, and splitting it up gets you answers faster.

Before you hit send, boil your ideas down to a clear vision of what you want made. Most of our work is custom priced, so the more detail you give us, the more accurate your quote.

If you have artwork, attach it. We usually need to see it before we can price the job properly. We've also got digital product cards on a range of items — ask and we'll send those straight through.

What to include

Sizes and quantities — the two things people forget most often, and the two things a price hinges on.

Your artwork — or a note that you need us to design it.

Your deadline — lead times vary a lot across products, so tell us if there's a date attached.

Delivery or pick-up — all orders are collect in-store from 44 McBean Street unless you tell us otherwise before ordering.

Step 2

We quote

Your enquiry gets analysed by the team who'd make the job. If we need more information, you'll hear from us — and that's usually because a size or a quantity is missing.

Here's the honest bit: we prioritise enquiries that have everything we need. "How much for some shirts?" goes to the bottom of the queue, because there's nothing there to price.

We're a busy walk-in shop as well as a production workshop, so if you need something sorted immediately, call us or come in. That's genuinely the fastest way to get a human on it.

Worth knowing

If you'd like a different set of options after you've received your quote, that can push you back to the start of the queue. We're not being difficult — re-pricing a job is nearly the same work as pricing it the first time.

We give priority to clients who know what they want and are ready to order. Come to us decided and you'll be looked after quickly.

Step 3

Process payment

Quote accepted, invoice generated. We require full payment before production starts — because we have yet to discover a way to put the ink back into the printers. Custom products can't be un-made.

For larger orders and corporate work we have deposit options, available on request. Just ask when you're quoting.

Delaying approval or payment delays your due date, so the smoothest orders are the ones checked, double-checked and paid promptly.

Garment & merch specific

Suppliers sell out of popular styles constantly. Our team confirms stock before offering a style in your quote, but a delay in payment can mean your size run is gone by the time it clears.

We don't make changes once your proof is approved and the job is in the production queue, and there are no refunds on custom-made goods. That's the trade-off with made-to-order.

Step 4

Artwork approval

Once your order is paid, we prepare an art proof using your supplied artwork and send it over for approval. Check it carefully — details, sizes, quantities, spelling, measurements.

If your files need changes or alterations to work in production, we'll tell you what's involved and what it costs before we do anything. No surprise line items.

If your enquiry is for design work, that's invoiced before the design team starts. They'll walk you through exactly how it works once your project is on board.

Change your mind early

We can change anything before approval. Once the job is in motion it's much harder — changes at that point push you back into the design queue and delay your due date.

Because we run a full design service, we don't do free artwork checks. If you're supplying your own files, ask us for an artwork spec sheet first so your creative juices don't run into a wall.

Step 5

Production & completion

This is the part where you sit back. We check the details, run the job, and quality-check it before it leaves the bench — print, merch, apparel, stickers, all of it.

Your order is completed according to the lead times stated on your approval. When it's done, we email you to say it's ready to collect from the shop.

Unless you've told us otherwise before ordering, everything is collect in-store at 44 McBean Street, Yeppoon.

Made here, checked here

Design, print, embroidery, garment printing and sticker cutting all happen under one roof with the same team. Nothing gets handed to a broker and hoped for.

If something isn't right, you're talking to the people who made it — not a call centre in another state.

Good to know

Common questions.

Do I have to pay before you make anything?
Yes — full payment is required before production starts. Everything we make is custom, and we have yet to discover a way to put the ink back into the printers. For larger orders and corporate work we have deposit options available on request.
Can I change my order after I've approved the artwork?
Once a job is paid for it moves into the production queue and we don't make changes. Before approval, change whatever you like. That's exactly why the approval stage exists — check the spelling, the sizes and the quantities properly at that point.
What if I don't like it when it's finished?
There are no refunds on custom-made goods — they can't be un-made or resold. What we can do is make sure you're happy before it's made: you sign off an art proof, and for embroidery we can stitch a physical sample first for a fee.
Do I get a free artwork check?
No. We run a full design service, and checking and fixing files is design work — so it's quoted, not given away. Ask us for an artwork spec sheet before you supply files and you'll almost certainly avoid needing any fixes at all.
When does my art proof happen?
After your order is paid. We then prepare an art proof from your supplied artwork and nothing is produced until you approve it.
Why do I have to fill in a form instead of just asking a price?
Because almost everything we do is custom priced, and a price without sizes and quantities is a guess. The forms make sure we get the details we need first time, which is genuinely faster for you. Enquiries with all the information get answered first.
Should I send one enquiry or several?
Several, if you want several different things. Print, apparel, stickers and promo are handled by different people — separate enquiries land with the right department and come back quicker.
Do you deliver, or do I collect?
All orders are collect in-store at 44 McBean Street, Yeppoon unless you tell us otherwise before placing the order. Choose delivery when you enquire and you'll get a shipping notification with a tracking number. We ship Australia-wide.
What if I need something urgently?
Call us on 07 4930 2574 or walk into the shop. We're a busy walk-in showroom as well as a workshop, and for genuinely urgent work a conversation beats an email queue. Just be aware that some products — sublimated shirts, custom caps, fully custom merch — can't be rushed no matter who you ask.

Know what you want? Let's get moving.

Sizes, quantities, artwork and your deadline. Send those four things and you'll be at the front of the queue.
