Good to know

Common questions.

Can I see and handle samples before I commit?
Yes — that's exactly what the showroom is for. Call into 44 McBean Street in Yeppoon and check the products yourself. For custom truckers we keep full physical swatch sets for fabric, mesh and stripes.
What's the difference between your curated range and the supplier catalogues?
The curated range is ours. We tested countless suppliers and manufacturers, then went direct with the ones that made the cut, and the garments are made to our specifications and patterns. The supplier catalogues give you enormous variety across everything else — bottles, lanyards, keyrings, umbrellas, glassware and so on.
How long does fully custom gear take?
Longer than you think, and deliberately so. Every shirt, polo, short and towel gets custom artwork, then samples are run before manufacturing. Custom-manufactured trucker caps take up to 60 days for a first-time order. If your event has a hard date, tell us up front and we'll be honest about whether it fits.
Can you put our logo on something we've already sourced?
For apparel we generally don't decorate customer-supplied items — and where we do agree, they must be brand new and unworn and a per-garment fee applies. For promotional products it's usually cheaper and cleaner to let us source the item as part of the job.
Are the coolers really Australian made?
Yes. We source Australian-made coolers, which means a faster delivery time and a better product than the imported alternative.
Do I get to see the artwork before it's produced?
Always. Once your order is placed we lay up your artwork and you approve it before anything is printed, stitched or manufactured. Nothing goes into production without your sign-off.
